# Citation

**Forgetting Without Restarting: Execution-State Unlearning for Stateful LLM Agents**. Yao, Chao; Wei, Yangbo; Huang, Zhen; Qian, Junhong; Chen, Chenle; Lu, Shaoqiang; Wu, Chen; He, Lei. First public: 2026-09-04; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04875v1); v1 (2026-09-04).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Remove influence from transcript, summaries, plans and cache

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Remove influence from transcript, summaries, plans and cache. Its scope matters because exactness needs deterministic replay and fixed observations.

## Method

Model the runtime as a deterministic transition system. Restore the checkpoint before the forgotten item, crop cached state and replay the suffix without it. The earliest injection determines a conservative tainted suffix unless finer attribution is available.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** Track runtime influence
- **Retrieve:** Reconstruct clean context/cache
- **Update:** Counterfactual runtime repair
- **Consolidate:** Rebuild summaries during replay
- **Invalidate:** Invalidate all tainted suffix artifacts
- **Forget:** Execution-state unlearning beyond record deletion

## Representation

Runtime checkpoints and provenance

## Controller / Decision Policy

Prefix restoration and suffix replay. API/local replay; can be costly for early deletions. Runtime cache access may not be exposed by hosted APIs.

## Datasets

100 LongMemEval, 100 ToolSandbox and 80 AgentDojo cases in controlled replayable setups.

## Baselines

Nine strategies including plaintext deletion, reset and cached-prefix replay.

## Metrics

Leakage probes, counterfactual action distance, utility, recomputed tokens and latency.

## Main Findings

Under the formal assumptions, exact unlearning requires at least T−tau+1 recomputed transitions and suffix replay attains it. Reported recompute gains are larger against full replay than against prefix-cached replay.

## Ablations

Cross-layer deletion/replay variants and leakage probes.

## What It Actually Solves

Remove influence from transcript, summaries, plans and cache. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Exactness needs deterministic replay and fixed observations. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

No parameter unlearning; no reversal of irreversible real-world actions or alternative human observations. Requires replayable runtime state.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

A non-significant difference from reset is not an equivalence proof. Selective branch repair needs trustworthy attribution; otherwise conservative suffix replay is necessary in the stated model.

## Relationship to Other Papers

ChronoMem restores global store snapshots; rollback repair preserves independent descendants under explicit lineage.

## Relevance to EvoMem
