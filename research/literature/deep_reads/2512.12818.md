# Citation

**Hindsight is 20/20: Building Agent Memory that Retains, Recalls, and Reflects**. Latimer, Chris; Boschi, Nicoló; Neeser, Andrew; Bartholomew, Chris; Srivastava, Gaurav; Wang, Xuan; Ramakrishnan, Naren. First public: 2025-12-14; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12818v1); v1 (2025-12-14).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Combine factual and experiential recall with belief-aware reflection

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Combine factual and experiential recall with belief-aware reflection. Its scope matters because confidence updates are not demonstrated calibrated probabilities.

## Method

Separate world facts, experiences, opinions and observations. TEMPR combines semantic, keyword, graph and temporal evidence; CARA uses reflection to update belief-like opinions and produce conditioned responses.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** Retain extracted facts and experiences
- **Retrieve:** TEMPR multi-strategy retrieval
- **Update:** Opinion/observation changes through reflection
- **Consolidate:** Reflect across accumulated evidence
- **Invalidate:** Not a complete logical revocation contract
- **Forget:** Selective forgetting is discussed as further work

## Representation

World, experience, opinion and observation networks

## Controller / Decision Policy

TEMPR retrieval and CARA reflection. Multiple API/local model options; no single verified matched all-system cost total.

## Datasets

LongMemEval and LoCoMo; configurations include GPT-OSS memory models and different answerers.

## Baselines

Reported results from other memory systems plus internal configurations; not all are controlled reruns.

## Metrics

QA/judge performance and memory retrieval analyses.

## Main Findings

Provides richer memory semantics than undifferentiated stored text. Opinion confidence and evidential reflection already weaken the claim that memory systems treat every item as equally trustworthy.

## Ablations

Component/model configurations in evaluation and appendix; comparability depends on answerer and retrieval budget.

## What It Actually Solves

Combine factual and experiential recall with belief-aware reflection. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Confidence updates are not demonstrated calibrated probabilities. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

Future directions include learned joint control, forgetting/privacy and richer revision.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

Heuristic confidence reinforcement is not calibrated probability. The inspected evaluation contains an unresolved retrieval-budget placeholder and mixed external baseline reports, limiting reproducibility and leaderboard interpretation.

## Relationship to Other Papers

BeliefMem explicitly notes uncalibrated scores; TRUSTMEM trains transition preferences.

## Relevance to EvoMem
