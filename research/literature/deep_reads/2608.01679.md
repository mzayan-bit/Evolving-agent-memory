# Citation

**When Memory Becomes Authority: Benchmarking Authority Collapse at the Memory Consolidation Boundary**. Zhan, Qiuyang; Zhang, Rui; Guo, Sheng; Zhao, Lepeng; Liu, Zhuotao. First public: 2026-08-03; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01679v2); v2 (2026-08-04).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Measure authority lost or upgraded during consolidation

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Measure authority lost or upgraded during consolidation. Its scope matters because source authority, truth and confidence are distinct labels.

## Method

Construct paired source-authority transformations and test consolidation, controlled downstream action and predicted authority labels. Separate user-authorized, assistant-attested and unendorsed tool evidence according to the benchmark policy.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** Consolidation/write probes
- **Retrieve:** Use written memory for actions
- **Update:** Authority transitions
- **Consolidate:** Central evaluated boundary
- **Invalidate:** Authority-preserving restrictions
- **Forget:** Not a broad forgetting benchmark

## Representation

Source-authority transitions

## Controller / Decision Policy

Paired write/action probes. API inference over paired examples; feasible baseline for source-authority preservation.

## Datasets

50 base histories, seven transitions each: 350 pairs. Entity-component split uses 30/10/10 base histories for train/validation/test.

## Baselines

Seven writer objectives across seven backbones; prompt adaptations, not seven fully native memory backends.

## Metrics

Authority upgrades, omissions, prohibited actions and benign task completion.

## Main Findings

48 of 49 writer/model configurations upgrade authority in at least some cases; the zero-upgrade exception omits all content. Selected predicted-label intervention reduces prohibited actions without losing the measured benign completions.

## Ablations

Writer objective, model, oracle/controlled labels and predicted labels.

## What It Actually Solves

Measure authority lost or upgraded during consolidation. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Source authority, truth and confidence are distinct labels. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

Benchmark-defined authority policy and selected downstream pipeline limit broader deployment claims.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

Authority is not factual truth or statistical confidence. Treat 350 transformations as clustered by 50 histories, not 350 independent histories. Prompt emulations do not establish product behavior.
