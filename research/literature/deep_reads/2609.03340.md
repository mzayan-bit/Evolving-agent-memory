# Citation

**Fresh Memory, Stale Plans: Dependency-Scoped Validation for Distributed LLM-Agent Memory**. Chen, Evan; Wang, Shiqiang; Brinton, Christopher G.. First public: 2026-09-03; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03340v1); v1 (2026-09-03).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Prevent execution of plans derived from outdated records

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Prevent execution of plans derived from outdated records. Its scope matters because complete dependencies and tool contracts are explicit assumptions.

## Method

Plans cite exact public records; the executor checks owner heads for dependencies that can affect an action. On revision or incomplete validation it replans once or blocks. Distributed coordination is scoped to relevant records.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** Not a general writer
- **Retrieve:** Fetch/validate dependency versions
- **Update:** Replan after relevant revision
- **Consolidate:** Outside scope
- **Invalidate:** Invalidate stale plan authorization
- **Forget:** Outside scope

## Representation

Exact plan dependencies and version heads

## Controller / Decision Policy

Dependency-scoped validation and replan/block. Frozen-model/API workflows plus low-cost deterministic replay; feasible baseline.

## Datasets

30 controlled live workflows in three domains; replay scales to 32,700 actions with churn and dependency perturbations.

## Baselines

Freshness-only execution, centralized lineage, eager synchronization and broad quorum validation.

## Metrics

Invalid actions, successful completion, validation checks, coordination stall and replay costs.

## Main Findings

All 30 deliberately revised workflows fail freshness-only execution and pass the gate; this is not natural failure prevalence. Low churn favors eager synchronization in coordination cost; high churn favors scoped validation.

## Ablations

Missing and extra dependency edges; Appendix C reports substantial unsafe execution when edges are omitted.

## What It Actually Solves

Prevent execution of plans derived from outdated records. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Complete dependencies and tool contracts are explicit assumptions. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

Excludes inferred dependencies, semantic merge, Byzantine participants and atomic check-and-act guarantees.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

The key novelty boundary is incomplete semantic lineage. Checking a perfect declared graph is already solved within this protocol. Extra edges can preserve safety by increasing checks, while missed edges undermine it.

## Relationship to Other Papers

## Relevance to EvoMem
