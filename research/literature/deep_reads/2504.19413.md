# Citation

**Mem0: Building Production-Ready AI Agents with Scalable Long-Term Memory**. Chhikara, Prateek; Khant, Dev; Aryan, Saket; Singh, Taranjeet; Yadav, Deshraj. First public: 2025-04-28; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.19413v1); v1 (2025-04-28).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Extract and manage compact conversational memories

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Extract and manage compact conversational memories. Its scope matters because update quality depends on extraction and candidate coverage.

## Method

An extractor uses recent conversation and broader summary context to propose memories. Similar existing records are retrieved; an LLM chooses ADD, UPDATE, DELETE or NOOP. The graph variant extracts entities/relations and handles graph conflicts.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** LLM extraction and selective insertion
- **Retrieve:** Dense candidate retrieval; graph variant
- **Update:** LLM CRUD adjudication
- **Consolidate:** Summaries supply extraction context
- **Invalidate:** Via update/delete; no general dependency contract
- **Forget:** Explicit DELETE

## Representation

Text memories; optional graph

## Controller / Decision Policy

LLM ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP over retrieved candidates. API calls per extraction and update plus embeddings; graph adds processing. Reported tokens/latency are protocol-specific.

## Datasets

LoCoMo released conversations; evaluation omits adversarial questions. Ten repeats are reported.

## Baselines

Full context, RAG, memory systems including Zep and A-MEM in the paper’s protocol.

## Metrics

F1, BLEU, LLM judge, token usage, p50/p95 latency.

## Main Findings

Shows an effective practical extraction/update pipeline and reports accuracy/cost advantages within its setup. Graph augmentation has additional overhead; no cross-paper universal ranking is justified.

## Ablations

Base versus graph memory and system comparisons; no complete oracle factorization of extraction, candidate recall and revision errors.

## What It Actually Solves

Extract and manage compact conversational memories. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Update quality depends on extraction and candidate coverage. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

Discussion identifies graph overhead and extensions beyond conversational/text settings.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

Nearest-candidate selection can miss a contradictory record. A correct CRUD decision on visible candidates does not imply a globally consistent store. Historical retention is not a safety invariant of destructive updates.

## Relationship to Other Papers

Memory-R1 learns a related CRUD policy; Zep emphasizes temporal graph retention; HaluMem evaluates update errors.

## Relevance to EvoMem

A required counterexample/baseline when considering extract and manage compact conversational memories. Any proposal must state its additional assumption, test and advantage over this work.
