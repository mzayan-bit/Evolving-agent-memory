# Citation

**Verifiable Memory: Learning Unified Memory Management with Local and Global Verifiers for Large Language Model Agents**. Sun, Xiaolong; Wang, Qichao; Li, Hangyu; Chen, Liang. First public: 2026-08-04; arXiv preprint; no publication venue independently verified. [Reviewed primary version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03137v1); v1 (2026-08-04).

Evidence level: targeted deep reading of method, evaluation and relevant limitations/appendices; no independent experimental reproduction. Author reports are distinguished below from reviewer inferences.

## Research Problem

Train unified control with local/global verification

## Why This Paper Exists

The design targets a concrete weakness in how prior agents evaluate or use memory. Train unified control with local/global verification. Its scope matters because episode-reset evaluation does not establish cross-session lifecycle safety.

## Method

One policy manages long-term memory, active context and episodic history. Seven tools cover add/update/soft-delete, retrieve/filter/summarize and selecting episodes. SFT precedes staged RL with local executable-transition and global coherence verifiers.

## Memory Lifecycle Covered

- **Write:** ADD
- **Retrieve:** RETRIEVE and SelectEpisode
- **Update:** UPDATE with new versions
- **Consolidate:** SUMMARY
- **Invalidate:** Soft-delete/version status
- **Forget:** DELETE/FILTER without permanent history erasure

## Representation

LTM, active context and episode history

## Controller / Decision Policy

Seven operations; SFT and RL. SFT plus multi-stage RL on 4B/7B-class models; total GPU-hours not reliably extracted here.

## Datasets

ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, PDDL, BabyAI and HotpotQA; memory resets each evaluation episode.

## Baselines

AgeMem and related memory/context methods under two Qwen backbones.

## Metrics

Task accuracy/success, average score and verifier/component analyses.

## Main Findings

Reports mean 48.01/59.85 versus AgeMem 41.96/54.31 under its matched settings. Unified learned control, versioned updates and explicit training verifiers are therefore not new project contributions.

## Ablations

Local/global verifier and policy-training components.

## What It Actually Solves

Train unified control with local/global verification. The empirical support is restricted to the tasks and controls described above.

## What It Does NOT Solve

Episode-reset evaluation does not establish cross-session lifecycle safety. This is a scope assessment, not a claim that the approach could never be extended.

## Author-Stated Limitations

Persistent cross-session, conflicting and adversarial memory robustness are not established by the evaluation.

## Additional Limitations We Observe

Verifiers used in training are removed at inference; reward shaping does not create a runtime semantic safety contract. Episode resets sharply constrain claims about lifelong accumulation.

## Relationship to Other Papers

AgeMem unified actions; TRUSTMEM transition feedback; MemTX runtime rather than training governance.

## Relevance to EvoMem
