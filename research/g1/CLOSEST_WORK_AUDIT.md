## Evidence that most narrows G1

Rollback already rescues independently supported candidates; PlanFence already sweeps missing and excessive dependencies. CUPMem already performs inferred persistent invalidation, including weak and unknown-current states. CAMA already infers latent source dependence and learns recovery under costs. StateAuditor already has matched controls, cyclic/ambiguous stress cases and a null hard-set accuracy result. A list combining their features is not an algorithmic contribution.

## Most threatening separating experiment

Take Rollback with its existing independent-support check; give all policies the same source history, source authority, fault cue and corrupted observed lineage. Evaluate three revisions with current and as-of probes plus delayed memory writes/retrievals. Cross missing versus spurious relations with true independent alternatives versus copies; freeze inference and verifier evidence. Compare ordinary closure, point-estimate support-set truth maintenance/rederive, CUPMem-style semantic reverify and full replay at the same repair/verification/token budgets. If the simple support-set or replay policy covers the frontier, no new uncertainty controller is warranted.
