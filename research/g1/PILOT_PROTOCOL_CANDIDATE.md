# Pilot protocol candidate v0.2 — NOT FROZEN, NOT EXECUTABLE

Prepared 2026-09-25. This candidate revises, but does not silently overwrite, the v0.1 preregistration. Human signatures, actual adapters and cost fields are missing. No real pilot is authorized. Existing 30 cases remain development only.

## Objective, questions and hypotheses

Characterize support-inference versus repair-decision errors in persistent state under revisions, with equal evidence and resources. Primary RQs: does controlled support stress change trajectory-macro stale reuse/preservation, and do strong classical/recompute baselines leave material headroom? Secondary: alternatives/copies, repeated history, transfer, and inference × policy attribution. H1 stress can increase stale reuse or collateral loss; H2 correct support sets preserve valid alternatives (classical prediction); H3 useful residual headroom MAY exist; H4 any effect survives natural errors and backbone changes. No claim that an unimplemented method wins.

## Data, splits and exclusions

Complete two independent human passes on the 30 drafts, agreement review, corrections and adjudication. Use them only for development. Candidate pilot size: 12 NEW scenario families, all evaluation after freeze; three revisions and fixed current, historical-then, retrospective, delayed reuse/action opportunities. Twelve is a bounded variance/cost diagnostic, not a powered positive/null study. This explicitly replaces v0.1's eight tuning/four held-out allocation, which would leave only four untouched evaluation units. No new cases are generated in this phase.

Before freeze exclude duplicate source families, unavailable licensed evidence, incoherent time/scope, and intended-determinate cases that cannot be adjudicated. Preserve genuine unresolved cases as a separate stratum; do not discard them because a policy struggles. After freeze retain all attempts: parse failures, cap exhaustion and wrong answers are outcomes. Infrastructure failures get missing-outcome flags and best/worst sensitivity bounds, not zero errors.

## Baselines and comparison classes

Nine engineering algorithms: B0–B7 and cached B8. Main strong threats B2/B6/B7/B8. B0/B1 are controls with currently identical derived outcomes; B3 naive closure and B4 lexical cosine cannot masquerade as published competitors. Before any method comparison require a common model inference/verifier adapter, defensible Rollback-with-independent-support/selective-replay adaptation (or attributable official implementation), CUPMem development integration, and query-only audit versus persistent-write comparison. No adapter inherits a paper name without a deviations ledger.

Plan trajectory count as `N × K × families × regimes × seeds`, never silently fix 384 after adding arms. Nine arms, 12 families, two backbones, two regimes, one seed = 432 trajectories. With three additional justified arms (Rollback adaptation, CUPMem, query-only control), K=12 gives 576. These are cost envelopes, not a run authorization or a requirement to spend that amount. Resolve adapter semantics first; reduce N or arm scheduling only before freeze with explicit scientific consequences. Never remove a strong threat because it wins development.

## Oracles, models and information

Separate O1 fault, O2 graph, O3 support sets, O4 complete past evidence and O5 replay ceiling per [oracle ladder](ORACLE_LADDER.md); not pooled in main rankings. Main condition uses observed source-change cues, common frozen inferred proposals and identical accessible evidence. Explicit executable fixture rules cannot become hidden gold semantic verification in natural data. No future versions, gold affected masks or judge labels at runtime.

Candidate families: current pinned Claude Sonnet 5 and Qwen3.5-9B, with a larger open-model sensitivity if affordable; exact access/runtime checks in [model plan](MODEL_PLAN.md). Compare policy effects within each family, not raw cross-model capability. Shared readout and query schedule; randomize policy order with a logged schedule. Shared setup/inference charged equally. Freeze initial memory and evidence bundles before policy runs.

## Noise, revisions and budgets

Pilot contrast: unchanged inferred proposal versus one combined synthetic missing/spurious regime, provisionally .7 true retention/.3 inserted per true incidence if development confirms meaningful masks. This is not gold completeness, natural-error prevalence or separate omission/insertion identification. Annotate mechanism strata and plan systematic/natural-error expansion before a method claim. Three revisions include legitimate supersession/reinstatement and delayed reuse where natural to each case; do not require every ontology type in every case.

Mandatory unrestricted and matched-resource views. Six released model-call credits (two per revision with carry) is a candidate reference, not sufficient matching by itself. Count every physical inference/diagnosis/verifier/replay request, retrieval and tokens, including failed calls. Freeze common input/output/retrieval limits, retry cap, timeout, monetary ceiling, source availability and cache policy using development-only measurements. Dollar ceiling, actual token caps and provider reservation implementation remain UNSET and execution-blocking. Deterministic replay-output steps are not comparable to model calls and cannot support a compute-savings claim.

## Outcomes, statistics and stopping

Primary: paired scenario-macro SDRR stress contrast, per strong baseline at the matched cap. Mandatory guardrails: FIR, valid quarantine/nonavailability, current and both historical accuracies. Secondary: alternative retention, recurrence, repair precision/recall, abstention/error among answered probes, reverify coverage, action correctness, actual cost/latency. Fixed opportunity denominators and null eligible rates follow existing metrics; no retrieval-dependent denominator.

Independent unit: source scenario family. Policies/backbones/revisions/masks/seeds/probes are repeated measures. One paired generation seed per pilot; record when provider seed is unsupported. At expansion use at least three seeds if stochastic variation warrants it, averaged within family. Bootstrap whole paired clusters (10,000 draws; percentile 95% interval; disclose instability at n=12); give per-family effects, eligible counts, macro/micro sensitivity and error bounds. No equivalence claim from a non-significant difference; Holm only for a predeclared confirmatory comparison family. [Sample-size plan](SAMPLE_SIZE_PLAN.md).

Stop on leakage, incoherent gold, unsafe budget enforcement, excessive infrastructure errors, or annotation gate failure. Preserve all logs. Kill method development if replay/TMS/conservative checks cover the frontier, gains disappear after matching, or naturalistic/strong-backbone tests erase the signal. Existing 5-point SDRR practical signal and 2-point guardrails remain provisional stakeholder tolerances, not literature-derived effect sizes; humans must approve them before freeze. Expansion needs reliable annotation, material stress effects/headroom with uncertainty, plausible costs and independent confirmation material. Wide intervals mean inconclusive, not success.

## Freeze checklist and readiness

Required signatures: annotation coordinator, experiment owner, independent audit reviewer. Freeze dataset/split/mask hashes, adapters with fidelity classifications, prompts, models, cost schema/pricing snapshot, settings, outcome/table specs, statistical script and stop rules. Missing model-backed competitors, query-only adapter, credit scheduler/reservation and natural-language evidence semantics mean **R0 for the proposed real study**, despite tested deterministic engineering. Human adjudication is necessary but not the only remaining gate.
