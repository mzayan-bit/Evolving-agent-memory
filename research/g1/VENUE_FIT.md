# Scientific framing and venue fit

These are analyst judgments about audience fit, **not acceptance predictions or claims about an upcoming submission deadline**. Consult the actual future call before submission. The official guidance below was inspected on 2026-09-21; venue guidance is not novelty evidence for the 2026-09-20 literature cutoff.

## Three possible framings

1. **Analysis/evaluation:** when semantic support uncertainty breaks persistent repair. Best supported direction to investigate now, provided humans can annotate the object and controlled effects generalize. It may legitimately produce a negative result about elaborate repair.
2. **Method:** uncertainty-aware selective repair. Not supported yet. Requires headroom over point support-set truth maintenance, full replay and conservative verification at matched cost, plus calibrated decision-relevant uncertainty if claimed.
3. **Protocol plus modest method:** a reusable support-revision evaluation and a clearly delimited decision improvement. Avoid bundling an elaborate benchmark with an unneeded controller; each must independently earn its contribution.

| Venue/audience | Contribution fit | Empirical and reproducibility expectations for this project | Current weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| ACL | Language-grounded support, temporal reference, semantic ambiguity and agent behavior | Human-valid ontology, strong NLP baselines, held-out linguistic/domain variation; release cases/prompts/splits/scoring and agreement | Current cases are AI drafts; no evidence that language ambiguity has a reproducible structure |
| EMNLP | Empirical language-model behavior and carefully controlled negative findings | Broad enough model/data comparison, error analysis, matched information/compute and human judge validation | Thirty development cases alone cannot establish external relevance |
| NAACL | Language understanding/agent memory with clear empirical insight | Same soundness and transparency bar; meaningful temporal/scope analysis and accessible reproduction | Not a substitute destination for weak novelty or incomplete annotation |
| COLM | Language-model mechanisms/behavior, post-training or inference/evaluation | Frozen models, contamination controls, convincing model-family transfer, code/data and cost transparency | No demonstrated LM-specific insight beyond classical maintenance yet |
| ICLR | General learning/inference insight or strong empirical analysis | Isolation of representation/inference/policy, generalization and reproducible ablations | A hand-built controller or ontology alone is weak evidence of a general learning contribution |
| ICML | General machine-learning principle, rigorous empirical/theoretical contribution | Strong matched baselines, uncertainty/statistical rigor and reproducibility; calibration claim must be tested | Current framing is closer to agent evaluation than a new ML principle |
| NeurIPS | Broad ML significance, evaluation methodology or dataset contribution | Generalizable insight and checklist-level disclosure; dataset framing requires documented provenance, limitations and responsible release | Constructed stress cases may be too narrow; no independent validation yet |
| AAAI | Reasoning, knowledge representation and agents | Explicit relation to TMS/ATMS/AGM, sound semantics and credible agent experiments | Classical novelty threat is especially visible to this audience |
| ACM SIGMOD/PVLDB | Data provenance, uncertain data and incremental maintenance | Only plausible if the work contributes data-management semantics/algorithms or a substantial systems evaluation with strong DB baselines and artifacts | Repackaged provenance polynomials on a small LM benchmark are insufficient |
| ACM TIST | Substantial integrated intelligent-systems study | Mature evaluation, reproducibility and practical evidence across settings | No system/result exists; a journal article is premature |
| IEEE TKDE | Knowledge/data engineering, provenance and maintenance | A substantive data/knowledge-engineering result with scalability, semantics and comparisons | Generic “IEEE” targeting does not resolve missing scientific contribution |

## Source basis versus judgment

[ARR reviewer guidelines](https://aclrollingreview.org/reviewerguidelines) and [ARR call](https://aclrollingreview.org/cfp) ground the ACL-family emphasis on clear contribution, sound evaluation and reproducibility, including negative findings. They do not guarantee a specific conference’s future policy. [ICLR reviewer guide](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuide) and [ICML reviewer instructions](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerInstructions) emphasize value/new knowledge, soundness and reproducibility. [NeurIPS checklist](https://nips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist) grounds disclosure/statistical expectations. [COLM call](https://colm.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers), [AAAI main-track call](https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-26/main-technical-track-call/), [ACM artifact review policy](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current) and [IEEE TKDE](https://www.computer.org/digital-library/journals/tk/cfp-ieee-transactions-on-knowledge-data-engineering) establish relevant scopes/artifact context. Row-specific fit/weakness assessments are our inference, not official venue requirements or acceptance forecasts.

Choose the science after annotation and baseline evidence. An honest negative analysis can be more useful than forcing a method framing.
