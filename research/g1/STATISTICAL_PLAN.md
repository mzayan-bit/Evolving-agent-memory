# Statistical plan (draft, no observations)

Experimental unit is the independent source scenario/trajectory family. Policies, backbones, corruption masks, revisions, probes and seeds are repeated measures within it. Shared-source adaptations or paraphrases belong to one cluster; do not inflate n by treating questions or copied scenarios as independent.

Primary estimand is paired trajectory-macro SDRR difference between stressed and complete-lineage regimes for each strong baseline, plus paired policy differences at the fixed matched-resource point. FIR and current/historical accuracy are mandatory guardrails. Preselect point support-set TMS and conservative reverify as primary baseline threats; all other pairwise comparisons are descriptive unless separately preregistered. Aggregate across backbones with equal weights only after publishing each separately. Micro opportunity-weighted rates are secondary and disclose whether long scenarios dominate.

For confidence intervals, resample independent scenario clusters with replacement, retaining every within-cluster policy/backbone/seed/regime measurement (10,000 bootstrap draws planned). Calculate paired differences within each draw; report percentile 95% intervals with sensitivity to small-n bootstrap instability. With 12 clusters, intervals and variance estimates are exploratory; do not interpret failure to reject as equivalence. Later sample size uses pilot scenario-level variance and a 5-point practical SDRR effect, with 2-point guardrails; freeze before collecting independent confirmation data.

Seeds: pilot one paired seed per scenario/backbone; later at least three model-generation seeds and fixed corruption-mask seeds if stochasticity warrants it. Seed API may be unsupported or only best-effort; record that and actual responses. Never claim deterministic replication from temperature zero. Average seeds within scenario for the primary cluster estimate; report between-seed variation.

Multiple comparisons: one primary stress contrast and a short predeclared baseline family. For any confirmatory family of multiple tests use Holm adjustment across that family; do not adjust selectively after inspecting p-values. Report all regimes and CIs including unfavorable ones. Significance testing is optional for exploratory pilot; effect size, uncertainty and practical margins decide expansion. Equivalence requires a prespecified margin and sufficiently narrow intervals, not a nonsignificant superiority test.

Failed runs remain in attempted-run denominators for current/action accuracy; report error/timeout/budget rates by arm. Repair outcomes unavailable after infrastructure failures are missing, not zero errors: give worst/best-case bounds and complete-case sensitivity. Deterministic infrastructure retries use a frozen retry rule and count cost. Algorithmic parse failures and exhausted budgets are outcomes, not excludable infrastructure noise. No removal based on incorrect answers, high costs or inconvenient ambiguity.

Stratify missing-only/spurious-only/combined, necessary/conjunctive/alternative/correlated, known/unknown fault cues, source availability and held-out domains when those regimes exist. Do not fit an elaborate interaction model on the first 12 cases. Report each metric's eligible scenario count, denominator, NA convention and interval method. Unknown support strata report coverage/risk/admissible actions rather than forced binary repair truth.
