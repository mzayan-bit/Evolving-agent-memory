# Negative-result commitments

| Outcome | Method decision | Potential defensible analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Full/cached replay dominates at realistic cost | Stop uncertainty-controller development | Characterize workload size/evidence availability where replay is sufficient; publish only if independent cases teach a reproducible boundary |
| Classical support maintenance dominates | No new method justified by AND/OR bookkeeping | Explain which noisy-inference assumptions matter and which classical representation solves them |
| Thresholded point inference suffices | Abandon probabilistic decision novelty | Study inference quality and minimal operational requirements, without relabeling a threshold as uncertainty reasoning |
| Human support agreement weak | Halt expansion; one predeclared rubric revision and fresh blinded pass only | Ambiguity/annotation limits may support a careful measurement paper; never force consensus using authored labels |
| Gains disappear at matched cost/evidence | Reject method advantage | Attribute gains to resources/evidence; resource scaling analysis may be useful |
| Gains exist only under random dropout | No general agent-memory method claim | Controlled robustness note only if useful; require natural/systematic errors for broader conclusions |
| Stronger backbone removes effects | Bound claim to model/workload; stop general claim | Capability-dependent failure boundary with transparent selection and costs |
| Query-only audit equals persistent repair | Drop persistence as core advantage | Quantify horizon/latency tradeoff; do not claim repaired state from a good answer |
| Intervals remain wide | Inconclusive | Report feasibility/variance; no equivalence or negative-effect claim |

Do not rescue sunk costs by selecting worse baselines, convenient severity, new metrics, easy templates or a weaker model. Null/negative analysis still needs reliable annotation, independent material, precise estimands, provenance and uncertainty. Merely obtaining no significant gain in 12 families is not a publishable conclusion by itself. Apply existing kill criteria with approved margins; candidate choices here do not change frozen claims after results.
