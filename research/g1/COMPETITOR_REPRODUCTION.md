## Adapters decision

No paper-method adapter is added in this phase. B8 is explicitly an internal classical computation analogue with `faithful_to_original=false`. A future Rollback adaptation must keep diagnosed fault and runtime provenance assumptions, independent trusted support, answer-relevant selective replay and irreversible-effect boundaries. StateAuditor query-only must stay query-only; persistent writes are a separately named extension. PlanFence belongs in an action-validation comparison; forcing it into persistent-state recall is misleading. CAMA needs its actual learned grouping or an explicitly non-faithful component ablation. These are prerequisites, not optional weak substitutes for a positive method paper.

## Paper-reported model settings versus runnable requirements

The Rollback paper's experiments use GPT-4o, with additional Gemini-3.6-Flash/Qwen3.6-27B; these are paper-reported names, not verified callable snapshots. PlanFence's appendix reports Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, temperature .2, top-p .95, output cap 1,024 and no response cache. CAMA reports DeepSeek-V4-Flash/Qwen3.6-27B plus trained evidence/recovery modules. StateMem reports DeepSeek-V4-Flash/Qwen3.5-9B; StateAuditor's main/supplement distinguish proposer, Claude generator and separate Gemini judging conditions. Exact executable dependency locks/API IDs cannot be established without their attributed releases. These settings do not substitute for reproducible code, trained checkpoints or endpoint access. Source locations: Rollback §5, PlanFence implementation appendix, CAMA experimental settings, and the already linked StateMem/StateAuditor competitor audits.

Portable-driver verification also recorded one **environment failure**: the launcher resolved the virtual-environment Python symlink to the base interpreter, where `openai` was absent. Invoking the venv path without resolving that symlink fixed the launch; the committed driver then passed all three assertions. Both attempts and runtimes are retained in `runs.json` with separate logs. This is not a CUPMem algorithm failure, and no API call occurred.
