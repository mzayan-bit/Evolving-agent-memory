# Stop rules, drafted before model evaluation

These are genuine gates. Thresholds below are **provisional practical tolerances**, not empirically validated constants. Humans must approve or revise them before pilot freeze; changes must be dated before seeing comparative outcomes. A wide confidence interval is inconclusive, not evidence of equivalence or success.

| Kill | Evidence needed | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Novelty | An attributable pre-cutoff work already makes the same persistent support-uncertainty experiment with independent alternatives, repeated revisions, calibration where claimed, preservation and matched resources | Kill the claimed contribution; only a materially different replication/analysis remains. Feature overlap alone has already killed broad first-method claims. |
| Ontology | After one rubric revision and fresh blinded annotation, persistent confusion among necessary/conjunctive/alternative/association; more than 20% of intended determinate cases cannot be adjudicated, or agreement remains below the guide’s gates | Stop expansion. Narrow claim to ambiguity analysis or abandon the benchmark. Never resolve by treating the AI draft as truth. |
| Baseline | Full replay, support-set TMS or conservative reverify is no worse within a 2-percentage-point stale-reuse margin, has no worse FIR/task completion and uses no more resources across the primary and held-out regimes | Abandon new-method development; report the simpler solution/boundary if scientifically useful. |
| Signal | Adequately precise uncertainty bounds show missing/spurious lineage changes SDRR by less than 5 percentage points and valid-retention/FIR by less than 2 points in the primary stress contrast | Stop the uncertainty-centered method direction. If intervals are wide, use variance/cost to decide a bounded extension, not a positive claim. |
| Method | Future method gains disappear under shared evidence/inference and equal resources, or exceed FIR by >2 points / reduce current or historical accuracy by >2 points | Reject the method claim. Separate inference/evidence benefit from decision policy. |
| Generalization | Effect exists only in author-built templates and reverses or stays below practical margins in independently authored held-out domains across both backbones | Stop a general claim; a narrow diagnostic analysis may remain. |
| Practical | Useful reduction requires expensive repeated checking while full source replay/requery is economically trivial at the observed workload | Stop complex repair development. Use measured cost, not a presumed benefit of persistence. |
| Persistence | Query-time auditing/fencing has equivalent future answer/action quality and lower total cost; persistent editing adds no meaningful benefit or harms historical access | Drop persistent repair as the central contribution. |

## Positive expansion gate

Proceed beyond a baseline-only pilot only if humans establish annotation reliability; lineage interventions produce meaningful effects; strong simple baselines leave demonstrable frontier headroom; and the required evidence is available at plausible cost. Target practical headroom: at least 5 percentage points lower SDRR with FIR/current/historical accuracy within 2 points, equal resources, and directionally consistent results across both model families and held-out cases. This is a target for judging value, not an anticipated result or pilot power guarantee.

No implementation of an uncertainty controller is justified by this phase alone. Current decision B applies to the research question. C is appropriate if robust failure analysis remains but only classical methods are needed; D if neither practical effect nor reproducible ontology survives.
