# Novelty threat matrix

Cutoff:19 September 2026. Risk concerns a proposed *contribution claim*, not paper quality. “Invalidated” means a broad first/absence claim has a clear counterexample. It does not imply the underlying problem is solved reliably in every setting.

## Preliminary claims actively rejected or narrowed

| Candidate claim | Closest primary work | Overlap / what already exists | Remaining distinction, if any | Novelty risk / decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Finalist-specific threats

| Contribution boundary | Most threatening papers | Exact overlap | Required differentiating experiment | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 uncertain-lineage persistent repair | Rollback repair + StateAuditor | Selective downstream recovery + implicit-premise inference | Missing/spurious lineage, alternative support, repeated revisions, false invalidation and equal cost | High overlap; medium residual confidence |
| G1 evidence uncertainty | CAMA + GovMem | Learned latent dependence, recovery and review-aware governance | Persistent post-update state repair, not just query arbitration/write promotion | High |
| G1 action safety | PlanFence + MemTX | Dependency gating, revocation and state transitions | Explicitly violate complete-lineage assumption; no inherited guarantee | High |
| G2 correction-preserving compression | OAS + RD-Forget + R3Mem | Budgeted compression, historical rescue and reconstructability | Unknown future corrections, total persistent bytes, no free raw archive | High |
| G2 witness preservation | SkillZip variants + SkillZip Pro | Contract/dependency preservation and deletion witnesses under persistent compression | Evolving factual support rather than extracted procedural contracts | High; domain substitution alone insufficient |
| G3 verification routing | MCB + MemCon + PlanFence + Router-Mem | Verify/ask decisions, learned control, version checks and stopping | Executed longitudinal source checks with withheld notifications and shifted volatility | High; low residual confidence |
| G3 freshness optimization | Classical refresh/age-of-information methods | Cost–freshness policies predate LLMs | Demonstrate headroom beyond TTL/hazard baselines and identify needed semantic information | High; adjacent review incomplete |

## Naming

“EvoMem” is only a repository working label. [Evo-Memory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20857) and [EvoMemBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18421) already occupy nearby names and concepts. Choose a precise final title after the empirical contribution is established.
